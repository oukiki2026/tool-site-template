About, privacy and contact

Maintained by OuKiki as an independent Kikomono project. No account, payment, advertising or affiliate link is configured here.

Local data

PDF and text inputs stay in the browser tab. Sudoku progress is saved in browser local storage and can be cleared by resetting a puzzle or clearing site storage. We do not receive your file contents or game entries. Hosting still handles ordinary requests and may log network information; local processing is not a promise of total anonymity.

Contact

Contact the operator. Report a reproducible problem using non-sensitive examples; do not send identity documents or private PDFs.
